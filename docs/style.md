# degenWars UI style (v2)

*Style pass, 25 Nov 2026. Live in `style-v2.html` + `codex-v2.css`. Source of the override layer: `tools/style-v2/v2.css` (page) and `tools/style-v2/codex-over.css` (Codex); `tools/style-v2/build.py` rebuilds `style-v2.html` from the current `index.html` (font link, font tokens, then appends `v2.css` at the end of `<style>`).*

The UI is the steel case the paintings sit in. It should never compete with the art: dark metal, bone type, thin rules. Colour is spent only where something happens.

## When gold is allowed
1. The slot cabinet's own brass: bezel, bulbs, marquee sign and lever. The cabinet is brass, so these stay.
2. One primary call to action per modal (`.btn.gold`: Done, Choose, Next level).
3. Wins and rewards: payline traces, symbol win glow, level-up stars, the CHOSEN tag, scrap payouts, reward toasts, Legendary rarity (`.rar3`).

Anything else that is gold is a bug. Panels, borders, labels, pills, the rail, trays, the incursion line, sockets and the Shop all stay steel, charcoal and ivory.

## Type system (3 families + brand signage)
Loaded with one Google Fonts `<link>` and `display=swap`. All numbers are tabular (`font-variant-numeric:tabular-nums` on `body`).

| Token | Family | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--f-disp` | Saira Stencil One | titles, unit/core/item names, theme name, big buttons (SPIN, BATTLE!, DEPLOY), reel stamps, boss name |
| `--f-ui` / `--f-num` | Barlow Condensed 400–700 | body, labels (700, caps, +0.2em tracking), all readouts and counters (700) |
| `--f-mono` | IBM Plex Mono 400–600 | market tape, seeds, stamp sub-lines, cache plate, Codex dossier text and stats |
| `--f-brand` | Limelight | the "degenWars" wordmark only (marquee neon, home hero). Fallback is the stencil face, never serif |

Scale: `--t-2xs` 10 (caps labels) · `--t-xs` 11 · `--t-s` 13 · `--t-m` 15 · `--t-b` 16 (body) · `--t-l` 18 · `--t-xl` 22 · `--t-2xl` 28 · `--t-3xl` clamp(44–72).

## Spacing, radii, borders
- Spacing: `--s1` 4 · `--s2` 8 · `--s3` 12 · `--s4` 16 · `--s5` 24.
- Radii are square and industrial: `--r1` 2px (tags, plates), `--r2` 4px (buttons, pills, slots), `--r3` 6px (panels, modals). Round shapes are only for real round hardware (core sockets, bulbs).
- Borders: 1px `--line` plus the `--edge` inset (a faint top highlight and a dark inner line). No double brass frames.

## Surfaces
- Price tags in the Black Market are manila paper tags. The Codex notes and NEW DOSSIER slip are ivory paper with a cadmium rubber stamp. The Core Cache is an olive military crate with an ivory stencil plate.
- Toasts are dark slips with a colour-coded left edge. The JS colours are remapped onto palette roles in CSS: reward to mustard, error to rust, warning to orange.
- Reduced motion: the global reduced-motion block still applies; the incursion "now" pip no longer pulses, and the BATTLE! call-out ring is static.

## Art (painted UI layer)
*Added 25 Nov 2026. 18 painted images in `art/ui-*.webp`, generated by `tools/gen-art.py` (helpers `tex()`, `plate()`, `uic()`; backup of the earlier file in `tools/gen-art.pre-ui.py`). CSS lives in `tools/style-v2/art.css` (appended after `v2.css`) and the Codex block at the end of `tools/style-v2/codex-over.css`. `build.py` now also writes `codex-v2.css`. Rebuild: `cp index.html tools/style-v2/ && python3 tools/style-v2/build.py && cp tools/style-v2/style-v2.html tools/style-v2/codex-v2.css .`. Contact sheet: `shots/ui-art.png` (`python3 tools/ui-sheet.py`).*

Rules: a texture always sits under a wash (dark on steel, light on paper), so text keeps AA contrast. Button plates are stretched to the button (`center/100% 100%`), so their painted edge wear reads as a bevel, and the CSS keeps the 1px border, top highlight and drop shadow. Disabled is the steel plate under a dark wash with `grayscale(1)` and dim #77786f text. Hover is `brightness(1.12)` and pressed is the 2–3px drop plus `brightness(.92)`. Art rules use `!important` on `background`, because the base and v2 button selectors are very specific.
