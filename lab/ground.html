Ground calibration

Far units:
Drag the 4 gold handles onto the painting's ground: the far pair sits where the walkable ground ends near the horizon, the near pair at the bottom (they may go past the picture edges to match the perspective). Grid lines should run along the ground like floor tiles. Then set the slider so the front Grunt looks the right size. The blue dashed horizon line sets how fast units shrink with distance (true perspective: put it on the painting's horizon; lower = stronger shrink). The dashed line is the deploy line.
